### Decisions I made (please confirm)

1. **The depreciation refusal applies only to assets created from a supplier bill.** Assets entered by
   hand keep 6.5's fallback (in-service date = purchase date). Applying it to every asset would change
   how every existing asset starts depreciating. Test: `test_an_asset_entered_by_hand_still_starts_from_its_purchase_date`.
2. **The asset screen pre-filled the in-service date with the purchase date** and saved it before
   starting depreciation, so the server guard could never fire from the browser. For a bill asset the
   field now starts empty. Rule-49 proof 16 shows that without this the walk fails.
3. **New guard, not asked for:** a bill asset's purchase_cost/date/reference/currency/entity cannot be
   edited from the asset screen. FIXED_ASSETS was debited by exactly that amount; a correction is
   reversing the bill.
4. **Reversing a bill soft-deletes the asset it created.** Reversal is refused once that asset has
   depreciation, an addition, a disposal or a transfer.
5. **Form options endpoint.** The Purchasing role (purchasing.view/manage only) cannot read accounts,
   parties, entities or cost centers. One endpoint, `/purchasing/bills/form-options/`, behind the bill's
   own permission, serves the bill form. Otherwise the 8.0.1 handler would show "no permission" on the
   bill screen for that role.
6. **Withholding per line is computed and shown** (D6/D12 "الاستقطاع المتوقَّع") but posts nothing. It
   posts at payment in 8.3.
7. **Expenses list.**
   - Sources: EXPENSE lines of POSTED bills, plus the direct-account lines of posted payment vouchers on
     an EXPENSE account.
   - Voucher rows are shown only to a user with vouchers.view.
   - Amount = what the expense account was debited (non-deductible VAT included).
8. **VAT on a line is non-deductible** when the line's switch is off, or when the code itself is
   non-deductible (the seeded SN). Purchase VAT always posts to VAT_INPUT, never to the code's own
   account (the same reasoning as vouchers/services.py:350-368: S's account is VAT_OUTPUT).
