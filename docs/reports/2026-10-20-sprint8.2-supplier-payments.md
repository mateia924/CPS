## What shipped

Backend
- VoucherAllocation is generalized with two nullable FKs, invoice and
  supplier_bill, and the CHECK constraint `voucherallocation_exactly_one_target`.
  - vouchers 0007 adds it NOT VALID.
  - vouchers 0008 runs VALIDATE.
  - vouchers 0009 only changes the line-type choices.
- Payment vouchers get two new line types:
  - BILL settles a supplier bill. It is validated when the voucher is
    created: payment only, posted bill, same entity, same supplier, and
    allocated <= bill balance. It is checked again at posting, under
    select_for_update on the bill.
  - ADVANCE posts to SUPPLIER_ADVANCES.
- FX sign fix for payments: `fx_gain = fx_diff_total if is_receipt else -fx_diff_total`.
  A USD 1,000 bill at 3.75 paid at 3.80 books a 50 loss, a debit to FX_REALIZED.
- AdvanceApplication (purchasing 0003; accounting 0045 adds
  ProducedBy.ADVANCE_APPLICATION):
  - an ADVANCE or ON_ACCOUNT payment line can be applied to a bill from the
    bill screen;
  - same currency only;
  - a journal entry at the bill's rate, with the FX difference to FX_REALIZED;
  - can be withdrawn with a reason, which reverses its entry.
- Bill balances are never stored. They are computed by subquery from active
  allocations plus active applications.
  - A bill with payments cannot be reversed.
  - Reversing the payment voucher reopens the bill.
  - A voucher with an active application cannot be reversed until the
    application is withdrawn.
- Pay-now (D4/D12) creates a payment voucher with one BILL line for the full
  balance and posts it through the normal voucher path (approval rule,
  treasury balance, bill lock).
  - It requires vouchers.post (403 otherwise).
  - It requires a treasury account in the bill's currency.
- Reports:
  - `/api/reports/aging/?side=payable`, using the same buckets from due_date;
    `side` is validated;
  - `/api/purchasing/payables-by-week/`;
  - the supplier statement now has open bills and links to each document.

Frontend
- Voucher screen:
  - bill and advance lines;
  - the supplier's open bills;
  - prefill from `?bill=`;
  - a named error if the open-bill list fails to load.
- Bill screen: payments table, the «سداد» link, the pay-now form, open
  advances with apply and withdraw.
- Aging: switch between receivable and payable; the print view honours it.
- Statement: open bills and document links.
- New screen: payables by week, with its nav entry.
- On the aging, statement and weekly screens a failed load shows a named
  error state, never an empty result.

## Spec deviations (docs/prompts/sprint-8.md, amended lines)

- D7: "the table in the repo is named `VoucherAllocation` (not `Allocation`),
  and its target is two nullable foreign keys, `invoice` and `supplier_bill`,
  with a database CHECK constraint 'exactly one target', not a generic key
  (amended 2026-10-20 with 8.2). Reason: the table carries money, the targets
  are a small closed set, and a generic key drops referential integrity and
  makes joins for aging and statements worse."
- D12, pay-now: "shipped in 8.2: from the bill screen it creates a payment
  voucher with a `bill` line for the whole remaining balance and posts it
  through the normal voucher path (voucher approval rule, treasury balance
  check, bill lock at posting all apply). It requires `vouchers.post` and a
  treasury account in the bill's currency. 'Due' = total minus payments and
  applied advances."
- Not built (outside what the repo supports today):
  - cross-currency advance application, meaning an advance in another
    currency from the bill;
  - cross-currency pay-now. The voucher screen does handle a payment in
    another currency, with "allocated in bill currency".
